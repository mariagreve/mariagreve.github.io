Innovation · Entrepreneurship · Regional development

Maria Greve

Assistant Professor
Utrecht University School of Economics

I study how historical and institutional legacies shape regional innovation, entrepreneurship and economic development. My research explores knowledge mobility, inclusive innovation and the long-term effects of post-socialist transformation.

At Utrecht University, I am Deputy Head of the Entrepreneurship Section. Alongside my research, I teach entrepreneurship and economic development and connect academic evidence with policy challenges through collaborative, practice-based education.

01 Research

Institutions, people
and places.

Historical legacies & regional development

How institutions, socialism and economic transformation shape innovation and entrepreneurship over time.

Knowledge mobility & inclusive innovation

How inventors’ migration and women’s access to education influence the development and distribution of innovation.

Entrepreneurial ecosystems & societal missions

How regional ecosystems, policy and collaboration support economic development and sustainability-oriented change.

My work combines historical data construction with causal inference, spatial analysis and network analysis.
